## 1. Summary of Manuscript

The manuscript investigates the operator-theoretic structure of physical perfect optical vortex beams (PVBs) with finite annular thickness $w > 0$ at the waist plane $z = 0$. Recognizing that realistic finite-width PVBs fail to form an orthogonal basis, the author casts the family $\{\Phi_{l,R}\}_{l\in\mathbb{Z},\, R\ge 0}$ into the framework of continuous Hilbert frames. The paper shows that the frame operator $S_w$ acts as an isotropic Gaussian spatial convolution $G_w * \cdot$, resulting in a vanishing global lower frame bound ($A = 0$) over $L^2(\mathbb{R}^2)$, and derives strictly positive frame bounds $A(w, k_{\max}) = \exp(-w^2 k_{\max}^2 / 2)$ and $B = 1$ on the Paley--Wiener subspace $\mathcal{H}_{k_{\max}}$ of bandlimited paraxial fields. It then formulates a Tikhonov-regularized canonical dual frame $S_{w,\alpha}^{-1}$, derives an exact finite closed-form expansion $c_{l,p}(R)$ for arbitrary Laguerre--Gaussian ($\mathrm{LG}_{p,l}$) modes with $p \ge 0$, quantifies radial nodal washout via a reconstruction fidelity $\mathcal{F}$, and analyzes discrete radial sampling $R_n = R_{\min} + (n-1)\Delta R$ through the $N \times N$ Gram matrix.

## 3. Assessment of Classical & Quantum Optics Utility

A major shortcoming of `p3_3/main.tex` is the stark mismatch between the manuscript's **broad physical claims** and its **actual body content**:

1. **Quantum Optics Claims Without Any Body Content:** The Abstract (L35), Introduction (L43, L61), and Conclusions (L406) explicitly promise *"practical design bounds for ... spatial quantum state tomography"* and cite high-dimensional quantum cryptography and entanglement experiments (`mirhosseini2015high`, `bavaresco2018measurements`, `erhard2018twisted`). Yet **Secs. II–V contain zero quantum equations, zero POVM or density-matrix expressions, and zero quantum protocols**—even the word *"quantum"* does not appear once between L63 and L400.
   - **Critical Physical Subtlety for IC-POVM Radial Tomography:** On a radial qudit subspace $\mathcal{H}_d^{(l)} = \mathrm{span}\{\mathrm{LG}_{p,l}\}_{p=0}^{d-1}$ at fixed $l$, single-plane ($z = 0$) PVB projections suffer both azimuthal phase erasure (across $l \neq l'$) and real-polynomial rank deficiency: because $c_{l,p}(R) \in \mathbb{R}$ spans only $2d - 1$ independent degrees in $R^2$, a single-plane PVB measurement yields rank $2d - 1 < d^2$ (e.g., PVB outcome rank $7/16$ for $d = 4$) and is **not** informationally complete (IC). Achieving an IC-POVM over radial qudits requires **multi-plane Gouy-phase diversity**, $\theta_m = -(2p + |l| + 1)\arctan(z_m/z_R)$, across $M \ge d + 1$ static passive branches. Specifically, for $d = 4$, $l = 1$, $w/w_0 = 0.25$ evaluated on a 40-point midpoint radial grid $R_k \in (0, 3.5\,w_0]$ under measure $R\,dR$ across $M = 5$ Gouy planes uniformly spanning $[0, 0.45\pi]$ with $E_{m,k} = \frac{1}{M} R_k \Delta R\, \ket{\phi_{m,k}}\bra{\phi_{m,k}}$ and residual dark/uncollected outcome $E_{\text{res}} = I - \sum_{m,k} E_{m,k}$ ($\lambda_{\max}(\sum_{m,k} E_{m,k}) = 0.9050 < 1$), full operator rank $16/16$ is restored, yielding $\lambda_{\min}(\mathcal{F}) = 0.1261$ and Cramér--Rao bound $\operatorname{Tr}[\mathcal{F}^{-1}] = 41.80$ (or $42.96$ without $E_{\text{res}}$) at the maximally mixed probe $\rho_0 = I/4$ over an orthonormal traceless Gell-Mann basis in a single passive shot vs. $d+1$ sequential SLM switches.
   - **High-$d$ QKD & SPDC Iris Invariance:** Constant ring radius $R$ prevents $l$-dependent aperture clipping side-channels in high-$d$ QKD and preserves Bell--CHSH entanglement under circular-iris truncation. For $R_0 = 1$, $w = 0.1$, and iris radius $R_{\text{iris}} = R_0$, exact radial integration yields uniform transmission $t_{1..4}^{\text{PVB}} = [0.4999, 0.4996, 0.4991, 0.4984]$ (preserving $S_{\text{PVB}} = 2.8284 \approx 2\sqrt{2}$), whereas peak-matched $\mathrm{LG}_{0,l}$ modes ($w_0 = \sqrt{2}R_0$) drop asymmetrically as $t_{1..4}^{\text{LG}} = [0.2642, 0.0803, 0.0190, 0.0037]$ (collapsing two-mode Bell violation on $(l=1,4)$ to $S_{\text{LG}} = 2.0532$ under single-arm circular-iris truncation, whereas symmetric two-arm irises yield $S_{\text{LG}} = 2.0008$ vs. $S_{\text{PVB}} = 2.8284$).

2. **Underdeveloped Classical Optics Utility (17 Qualitative Lines in Sec. V):** Section V (`sec:tradeoffs`, L383–400) consists of two short qualitative bullet lists with zero quantitative calculations:
   - **No Worked Noisy Reconstruction:** Sec. IV.A–B derive $S_{w,\alpha}^{-1}$ and $c_{l,p}(R)$ but never evaluate them on a noisy test field. Quantifying the exact Fourier bias--variance trade-off on a concrete mode (e.g., $\mathrm{LG}_{2,1}$ with $w/w_0 = 0.25$) makes the value of $\alpha > 0$ immediately tangible while correcting L299's false zero-distortion claim.
   - **Unquantified RCF Coupling & SDM Equalization Penalty:** Sec. II.B (L102) misattributes $>99.5\%$ theoretical coupling (`rojas2021evaluating`) to the 300 m ring-core fiber (RCF) experiment (`aleman2023transmission`, i.e. Villalba *et al.* 2023, which achieved $6.83\,\text{dB}$ coupling gain over LG at $l=4$). Sec. V (L391) claims *"Zero-Forcing or MMSE equalization with minimal noise penalty"* without computing any penalty from Table II. Explicitly computing the normalized Gram zero-forcing (ZF) noise-enhancement factor reconciles both noise models: at $\Delta R/w = 2.0$, correlated projection noise yields $\frac{1}{N}\operatorname{Tr}(\gamma^{-1}) = 1.032$ ($0.14\,\text{dB}$) while post-projection white AWGN yields $\frac{1}{N}\operatorname{Tr}(\gamma^{-2}) = 1.099$ ($0.41\,\text{dB}$), dropping to $1.003$ ($0.01\,\text{dB}$) and $1.010$ ($0.04\,\text{dB}$) at $\Delta R/w = 2.5$.
   - **Unsubstantiated Turbulence Claim & Missing $z > 0$ Propagation:** L390 asserts *"small-scale atmospheric turbulence are naturally smoothed by $G_w(\mathbf{r})$"* without evaluating the RKHS projector $P_{\text{RKHS}} = \mathcal{T} S_w^{-1} \mathcal{T}^*$ on Kolmogorov phase screens vs. $D/r_0$, and Sec. II.A (L68) restricts the entire paper to $z = 0$ without commenting on Fresnel diffraction or conical self-healing $z_{\text{heal}} \approx R/\sin\gamma = k_0 R / k_r$.
