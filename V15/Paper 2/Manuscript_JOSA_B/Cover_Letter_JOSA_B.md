Dear Editor-in-Chief,

I am pleased to submit the enclosed manuscript, *"Classical and quantum photonic protocols based on physical perfect optical vortex beam frames,"* for consideration as a Research Article in the *Journal of the Optical Society of America B*.

Building on my co-authored experimental telecom ring-core fiber (RCF) demonstration [Villalba *et al.*, *Opt. Express* **31**, 40113 (2023)], our recent OAM covariance analysis [Ferreira *et al.*, *J. Opt.* **28**, 095601 (2026)], and the continuous Hilbert frame foundation established in my separate theoretical preprint [Ref. 27], this article translates finite-width PVB frame operators into four quantitatively benchmarked classical and quantum optical protocols:

1. **Step-Index and Parabolic Graded-Index (GRIN) RCF Coupling, Launch Tolerances, and Multi-Ring SDM:** Solving the Liouville-symmetrized Sturm–Liouville eigenvalue problem for both step-index and parabolic GRIN RCF profiles explains from first principles the experimentally observed $l = 0\text{--}6$ guided GRIN window ($99.11\%$ mean PVB coupling, $+6.97\,\mathrm{dB}$ gain over fixed-collimator $\mathrm{LG}_{0,4}$ vs.\ $+6.83\,\mathrm{dB}$ measured in Villalba *et al.*), quantifies $>91.5\%$ launch robustness across $\pm 0.5\,\mu\mathrm{m}$ radial and lateral offsets, and establishes $\le 0.41\,\mathrm{dB}$ AWGN zero-forcing penalty across $N = 6$ concentric SDM rings.
2. **Kolmogorov Turbulence Filtering vs.\ Matched-Dimension Optimal $\mathrm{LG}_{p,1}$ Projectors:** Single-OAM discrete PVB dual-frame projection suppresses field MSE by up to $5.77\times$ on single subharmonic Kolmogorov screens and $3.82\times\text{--}7.70\times$ across 5-screen split-step volume propagation, outperforming an optimally waist-tuned matched-dimension $N = 5$ $\mathrm{LG}_{p,1}$ ($p = 0\text{--}4$) projector at every turbulence strength.
3. **Passive Multi-Plane Gouy-Diverse Radial-Qudit IC-POVM Tomography:** Using five passive propagation planes with $\sigma_m^{+1}$-scaled wavefront-compensated PVB analyzers lifts single-plane rank from $7/16$ to full IC rank $16/16$ for $d = 4$ radial qudits ($\mathrm{CRB} = 41.80$ vs.\ $18.75$ for sequential 5-MUBs). Iterative $R\hat{\rho}R$ MLE reconstructs coherent radial superpositions to fidelity $F = 0.9823$ and 20 Haar-random pure states to mean fidelity $0.9922$ at $10^4$ photons, while maintaining $F \ge 0.972$ under $5\%$ uncalibrated axial plane positioning jitter.
4. **Circular-Iris Bell–CHSH Entanglement Preservation and Detection-Loophole Threshold:** Extending sixth-order $(w/R_0)^6$ Tsirelson-bound preservation into the high-transmission regime shows that physical PVBs clear the symmetric $82.84\%$ CHSH detection-loophole threshold at $R_{\mathrm{iris}} = 1.0476\,R_0$ ($99.994\%$ two-photon survival at $1.20\,R_0$), requiring $1.60\times\text{--}6.39\times$ less pupil area than three distinct Laguerre–Gaussian waist calibrations.

This manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration by any other journal. There are no conflicts of interest, and all simulation scripts and numerical benchmark datasets are openly available on GitHub/Zenodo.
