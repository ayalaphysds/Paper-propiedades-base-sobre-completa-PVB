FIX-03 [OPTIONAL] — Rigorous justification of non-compactness in Prop. 1
================================================================================

Location: Proposition 1 (prop:conv_kernel), final sentence of the proof
          (currently reads "...non-compact on unconstrained $L^2(\mathbb{R}^2)$
          by translation invariance, though Hilbert--Schmidt and compact
          when spatially restricted to any bounded pupil)").

Current phrasing ("by translation invariance") is heuristic. The rigorous
reason is that the symbol has uncountable essential range. Replace ONLY
the parenthetical:

Before:
  (non-compact on unconstrained $L^2(\mathbb{R}^2)$ by translation
   invariance, though Hilbert--Schmidt and compact when spatially
   restricted to any bounded pupil)

After:
  (non-compact on unconstrained $L^2(\mathbb{R}^2)$ because its symbol
   $e^{-w^2 k_\perp^2/2}$ has uncountable essential range $[0,1]$,
   whereas a compact self-adjoint operator's spectrum is at most
   countable with $0$ as the only possible accumulation point; it is
   Hilbert--Schmidt and compact when spatially restricted to any
   bounded pupil)

Rationale: a referee may query "why does translation invariance imply
non-compactness?". This removes the query. Optional.
