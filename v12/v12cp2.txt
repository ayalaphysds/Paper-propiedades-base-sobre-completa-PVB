BLOCKING-1 [REQUIRED] — "Rank 7/16" justification in §V.A
================================================================================

Location: §V.A (subsec:povm), paragraph beginning "Consider quantum state
          tomography on a d-dimensional fixed-l radial photonic qudit space..."

Current text:
  "single-plane projectors |Φ_l,R⟩⟨Φ_l,R| have real matrix elements in the
   LG_{p,l} basis and vanish on all d(d-1)/2 imaginary antisymmetric
   coherences, spanning at most 2d-1 < d^2 real Hermitian parameters
   (certified rank 7/16 at d = 4, l = 1)."

Problem: The reasoning gives "at most 2d-1 = 7" (for d=4), which matches
         the numerator of 7/16. But the text is confusing because 2d-1 = 7
         does not equal d(d+1)/2 = 10 (real symmetric dimension). The
         discrepancy means either:
           (a) The c_{l,p}(R) span only a 7-dimensional real subspace
               (not the full 10-dimensional real-symmetric space), OR
           (b) The rank should be reported as 10/16, not 7/16.

Fix Option A — If empirical rank is 7/16:
  Replace the parenthetical with:
    "(certified rank 7/16 by numerical SVD of the frame matrix at d=4, l=1;
     the reduced rank below the real-symmetric d(d+1)/2 = 10 reflects the
     specific analytic structure of c_{l,p}(R) for the low-p modes of
     LG_{p,1})."

Fix Option B — If numerical rank is actually 10/16:
  Replace "certified rank 7/16" with "certified rank 10/16" throughout
  §V.A and the abstract, and recompute the downstream claim "lifting
  single-plane rank 7/16 to full rank 16/16" as "lifting 10/16 to 16/16."

DECISION REQUIRED: Re-run the numerical SVD of the single-plane frame matrix
at d=4, l=1, and report the actual rank. Then apply Option A or B accordingly.

BLOCKING-2 [REQUIRED] — "18.75" MUB CRB derivation in Appendix A
================================================================================

Location: §V.A (subsec:povm), paragraph beginning "compared against 18.75
          for 5 sequential mutually unbiased bases (MUBs)"

Current text:
  "compared against 18.75 for 5 sequential mutually unbiased bases (MUBs)---
   trading a 2.23× photon-budget factor for completely passive,
   reconfiguration-free single-shot acquisition."

Problem: The value 18.75 is asserted, not derived. Given that the 2.23×
         tradeoff claim depends on it, this must be justified.

Fix — Add to Appendix A (app:methods), after the IC-POVM Fisher matrix paragraph:

  "For the comparison value Tr(F^{-1}) = 18.75 quoted in §V.A, we use the
   standard result for state tomography of a d-dimensional qudit with d+1
   MUBs under the Frobenius (generalized Gell-Mann) parameterization
   [CITE: e.g., Wootters & Fields 1989, or Bengtsson & Zyczkowski
   'Geometry of Quantum States' Ch. 3, or Scott 2006 for the d=4 case].
   For d=4 with 5 MUBs and uniform distribution of N photons across
   them, the asymptotic Fisher matrix has Tr(F^{-1}) = 15 × (d+1)/N =
   75/N, giving 18.75 at N=4 (per unit photon budget). The PVB POVM
   gives 41.80 at N=1 per-shot, so the tradeoff is 41.80/18.75 = 2.23×
   in photon budget for reconfiguration-free passive acquisition."

  If the actual reference or derivation differs, insert the correct version.
  If the number 18.75 cannot be derived from a standard reference, replace
  it with a computed value and re-state the tradeoff ratio accordingly.
